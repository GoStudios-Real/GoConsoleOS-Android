# GoConsoleOS Cast

## Short description
Mirror your Android screen to your GoConsoleOS console or TV over your home network.

## Full description
**GoConsoleOS Cast** lets you send your phone, tablet or TV screen to your
**GoConsoleOS** console — perfect for sharing gameplay, streaming media or
presenting across the room.

Built on the same LAN transport as the rest of the GoConsoleOS ecosystem:

- **Screen mirroring** — capture and stream your device to the host
- **Low-latency LAN streaming** over TCP :39101
- **Cast audio + video** frames between device and console
- Works with Android TV (Leanback) and handheld Android devices

### How it works
1. Open GoConsoleOS Cast from GoConsoleOS Portable.
2. Allow screen capture when Android asks.
3. Pick your console on the LAN — your screen starts mirroring.

Everything stays on your home network. No cloud, no third-party server.
