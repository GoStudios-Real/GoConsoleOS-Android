# GoConsoleOS GoAI

## Short description
GoAI — the offline gaming assistant built into GoConsoleOS. Ask it about games, USB health, performance and more.

## Full description
**GoAI** is the gaming assistant inside **GoConsoleOS**. Ask it anything about
your console and it answers instantly — engineered to run **fully offline**, so
your questions never leave your device.

- **Games** — "play Snake", or "recommend a game"
- **USB health** — "check my drives"
- **Performance** — "how's my RAM?", "what FPS am I getting?"
- **Account** — manage your GoConsoleOS ACC account
- **Cast** — "mirror my screen to the TV"

GoAI understands intent and answers from a local knowledge base. No cloud
account, no tracking, no internet required.

### How it works
Ask in plain English. GoAI replies with an answer and quick actions you can
tap to do the next step — launch a game, open USB health, or start a Cast.

Part of the GoConsoleOS companion suite and available on the host console too
(Settings → GoAI).
