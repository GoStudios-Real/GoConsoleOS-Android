# GoConsoleOS Portable

## Short description
Your gaming console in your pocket — discover, launch and manage a GoConsoleOS on USB, straight from your phone or TV.

## Full description
GoConsoleOS Portable is the companion app for **GoConsoleOS** — a
controller-first gaming console shell that turns a Windows PC (or USB stick)
into a living-room console.

GoConsoleOS Portable finds your console on the same Wi-Fi network and opens the
**Portable App** panel, a full launcher for every host tool:

- **GoUsbMaker** — build a Portable USB Gaming Console
- **USB Health** — SMART health reports for every USB drive
- **GoConsoleOS Cast** — screen-cast your device to the console or TV
- **GoAI** — ask your assistant anything, fully offline
- **GoStore** — browse the curated app catalogue
- **Screenshot** — capture the console screen

It also runs its own on-device **ACC** account service and **GoAI** assistant,
just like the desktop console — no cloud account, no data leaves your home.

### Key features
- One-tap discovery of GoConsoleOS hosts on your LAN
- Full remote tool panel for your console
- Native Android TV (Leanback) support
- Works offline, on-device and on-network only

The app connects over UDP :39100 (discovery) and TCP :39101 (control/stream)
with the GoConsoleOS desktop host.
