# GoConsoleOS USB Health

## Short description
SMART-style health checks for your portable USB gaming console and every USB drive attached to it.

## Full description
**GoConsoleOS USB Health** protects your portable gaming setup. It inspects
the storage behind your **GoConsoleOS** USB console and every USB device on
the network, and reports drive health before a drive failure can end a game
session.

- **On-device checks** — volume health, free space, mount status on your
  Android 13/16 device
- **Remote host reports** — the console's deep SMART report for portable USB
  drives, pulled over the GoConsoleOS LAN transport
- **Health score** per drive: OK / Fair / Poor / Unknown, with issue details

### Why it matters
Portable gaming consoles live on USB flash drives — the most failure-prone
part of the setup. USB Health tells you when a drive is degrading so you can
back up and replace it early.

Works with Android 13 (API 33) and Android 16 (API 36); runs on API 23+.
