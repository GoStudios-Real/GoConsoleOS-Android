# GoConsoleOS Link

## Short description
Browse and launch your PC's game library from your phone or TV — Steam Link style, fully local.

## Full description
**GoConsoleOS Link** turns your Android phone, tablet or TV into a second
screen for your **GoConsoleOS** console.

After the app discovers your console on the same Wi-Fi, Link pulls the full
game catalogue from the host and lets you:

- **Browse** every game in your GoConsoleOS library
- **Launch** any title remotely — the console starts it for you
- **Control** the console over LAN with the included input transport

Like every part of GoConsoleOS, it runs entirely on your local network. No
cloud, no account, no game data leaving your home.

### How it works
1. Open GoConsoleOS Link (GoConsoleOS Portable is required on the console PC).
2. Connect to your console when it appears on the LAN.
3. Tap any game to start it on the console.

Links into the GoConsoleOS **streaming transport** — control messages over
TCP :39101, matching the Windows host.
